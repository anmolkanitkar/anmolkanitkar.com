Getting started

Open Survibe and answer a few short questions; it suggests where to start in the course and a few songs. Every song works the same way: Listen first, then Practice (Survibe waits for each note), then Play with the band.

Playing on a real piano

The on-screen keys always work. To play on a digital piano or keyboard, connect it to the iPad with a USB cable, or pair a Bluetooth MIDI keyboard in Survibe's Settings › Keyboard. Survibe can't hear an acoustic piano.

No sound?

Check the iPad's volume and that it isn't on silent, then use Test sound in Survibe's Settings › Sound.

Language

Survibe speaks English, Hindi and Marathi. Change it in Settings › Language.

Your data

Survibe collects nothing; your progress stays on your iPad. See the privacy policy.
