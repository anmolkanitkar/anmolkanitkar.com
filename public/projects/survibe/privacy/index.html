Survibe for iPad

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Survibe collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app never sends anything over the internet. Everything you do in Survibe stays on your iPad.

What Survibe stores, and where

To remember your progress, Survibe keeps a few things on your iPad, inside the app's own storage:

  • the name you give, if you give one (it's optional), and your answers to the welcome questions, used to suggest where to start,
  • lessons and songs you've finished, your stars, streak and achievements,
  • your settings, such as language, keyboard sound, notation and reminder time.

None of this leaves your iPad, and nobody else, including the developer, can see it.

How long it's kept, and how to delete it

It's kept until you delete it. Deleting Survibe from your iPad deletes all of it at once. To start over without deleting the app, use Show the welcome questions again in Survibe's Settings.

If you back up your iPad to iCloud or a computer, that backup includes Survibe's data, like any other app's, and is handled by Apple or your computer, not by Survibe.

What Survibe does not do

  • It does not use the microphone or camera.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts or photos.
  • It does not connect to any server, and contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it has none to share.

Keyboards, Bluetooth and reminders

If you connect a MIDI keyboard, Survibe reads the notes you play so it can follow along. They are used on the spot and not recorded.

Survibe asks for Bluetooth only when you choose Pair a Bluetooth keyboard in Settings, and uses it only to find and connect that keyboard.

If you turn on the daily practice reminder, it is scheduled by your iPad itself and shows no personal details.

Children

Survibe is for learners of any age. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

TestFlight

If you're testing Survibe through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy. Survibe itself adds nothing to that.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if Survibe gains a feature that needs the internet, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
